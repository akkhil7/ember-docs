Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a memorial profile?

To create a memorial profile, tap the "+" button on the home screen and follow the onboarding process. You'll be asked to provide information about your loved one, including their name, relationship to you, photos, and memories.

How does the AI chat feature work?

The AI chat feature uses the information you provide about your loved one to generate conversational responses. The AI learns from the memories, personality traits, and details you share to create meaningful conversations. Remember that AI responses are generated and based on patterns, not actual knowledge of your loved one.

What's the difference between free and premium?

The free tier includes basic features with usage limitations (limited chat messages, memorial profiles, and storage). Premium subscriptions provide unlimited access to all features, including unlimited chat messages, photo analysis, story generation, and more storage space.

How do I manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your device's App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android) settings. You can cancel or modify your subscription at any time through these settings. Changes will take effect at the end of your current billing period.

Can I export my data?

Yes, you can request to export your data. Contact us through the app's support feature, and we'll help you export your memorial profiles, photos, and memories in a portable format.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account through the app settings. Once deleted, your personal information will be removed within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

Is my data secure?

Yes, we take data security seriously. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in secure cloud infrastructure, and protected by industry-standard security measures. We use secure authentication and access controls to protect your information.

What should I do if I encounter a bug or issue?

If you experience any technical issues, please contact us through the app's support feature. Include details about what happened, when it occurred, and what you were trying to do. This helps us diagnose and fix the issue quickly.

Account Issues

If you're having trouble with your account:

  • Forgot password? Use the "Forgot Password" option on the login screen
  • Can't receive OTP? Check your email spam folder and ensure your email address is correct
  • Account locked? Contact us through the app's support feature

Feature Requests

We love hearing from our users! If you have ideas for new features or improvements, please share them through the app's support feature. Your feedback helps us make Ember better for everyone.

Privacy and Data
